# Product specification

- Portrait-first, one-handed play; mouse and keyboard also supported.
- Five lives per level, no account energy gate.
- Paddle movement defines the first launch angle.
- Bonus balls never carry into the next campaign level.
- 10 worlds × 50 levels; mini boss every 10 and world boss every 50.
- Normal levels have no hard time limit.
- Lightweight permanent progression is disabled in competitive modes.
- Credits fund progression; crystals fund cosmetics.
- No pay-to-win products or forced banner ads.
- Email, Google and Apple accounts; no guest accounts.
- Follow, mutual friendship, global/friend leaderboards and shared replays.
- Weekly leagues with equalized stats.
- User-made levels use approved blocks/themes only and require validation/moderation.
- Turkish and English product structure.
- 13+ safety model without private messaging, location or user-uploaded profile images.
